City Council Votes 5-4 to Approve Downtown Zoning Change

By Maria Santos
May 11, 2026
photo.jpg
Council members during the vote.

The Lakeshore City Council voted 5-4 late Thursday to approve a controversial rezoning of the downtown commercial district, overriding objections from neighborhood groups who argued the plan prioritizes developers over residents.

The plan would allow mixed-use development on a 12-block stretch of Grand Avenue, replacing current height restrictions with a tiered system that permits buildings up to 12 stories near the transit corridor.

"This is a generational investment in downtown," said Council President Amara Obi, who cast the deciding vote. "We can't freeze this city in 1985 and expect it to thrive in 2030."
